# Website imagery

## New illustrative visuals

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. These are illustrative brand visuals,
not photographs of the manufacturer's premises or actual formulation.

- `farm-3840.webp`: cattle feeding at an open-sided Indian dairy farm.
- `botanicals-hd-3840.webp`: botanical ingredients and a stone mortar.
- Matching 640, 1280 and 1920 pixel variants provide responsive delivery.

The generated source images are 1672 × 941. The 3840 × 2160 versions are
upscaled exports, not native 4K photographs. The original farm and ingredient
brochure crops have been replaced, rather than merely enlarged.

### Final generation briefs

Farm: photorealistic editorial image of three brown and cream Indian dairy
cows feeding on fresh green fodder at a clean open-sided Maharashtra-style
farm; natural early morning light, earthy colours, nearest cow on the right,
realistic anatomy and fur; landscape crop; no labels, logos or people.

Botanicals: photorealistic editorial still life of fenugreek seeds, turmeric,
amla and ashwagandha roots beside a stone mortar on warm limestone;
soft side lighting, natural textures, understated green and ochre colours;
no labels, packaging, people or claims of a particular formulation.

## Original brand assets

`product-original.webp` preserves the supplied packaging photograph at its
original cropped resolution, 855 × 1010. No label text has been regenerated.
The hero uses an outline mask and renders the pack smaller for better clarity.

`logo-hd.webp` preserves the supplied logo at 900 × 800.

Native 4K packaging photography or the original label artwork is required for
true 4K product detail. Upsampling cannot recover absent printed detail.

## Final prompts

Common brief: one isolated photorealistic botanical ingredient cutout, compact centered arrangement, soft even daylight, crisp natural textures, transparent alpha background, no text or labels, no added unrelated ingredients, consistent premium editorial treatment.

### shatavari

Shatavari, Asparagus racemosus: a compact graceful cluster of long slender cream-beige tuberous spindle-shaped roots tapering at both ends, with one small sprig of fine feathery needle-like green foliage. Roots are the main subject. Not a ginger rhizome, not ginseng.

### ashwagandha

Ashwagandha, Withania somnifera: a compact group of dry light beige woody roots with modest branching, next to one short sprig of broad oval green leaves with two small orange-red berries partly enclosed in papery calyx. Roots are the main subject. Not shatavari tubers.

### fenugreek

Fenugreek: a small low mound of angular golden tan fenugreek seeds beside a fresh fenugreek sprig showing small distinct trifoliate green leaves. A few scattered seeds in front. No bowl.

### turmeric

Turmeric: three fresh turmeric rhizomes with tan-orange textured skins, one cut rhizome displaying rich vivid orange flesh and two orange slices, with a small narrow green leaf behind. Not pale ginger. No bowl.

### amla

Amla, Indian gooseberry: three fresh pale green round fruits with characteristic subtle vertical segment grooves, one half fruit showing the radiating interior segments, and a tiny delicate green foliage sprig. Not lime, not apple, not grapes.

### harad-ajwain

Harad and ajwain together: two dried brown oval haritaki (Terminalia chebula) fruits with distinct longitudinal ribs and wrinkled surfaces, one cut piece, alongside a clearly separate small mound of much smaller gray-brown ridged carom (ajwain) seeds. Show BOTH ingredients at realistic relative sizes. No large fresh citrus fruit.
